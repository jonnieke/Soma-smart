## Question paper

### Question 1 — 4 marks

Evaluate (-18 + 30) ÷ (-3) + 7. Show each stage of your calculation.

### Question 2 — 4 marks

A solid cube has a volume of 3375 cm³. Calculate (a) the length of one edge and (b) its total surface area.

### Question 3 — 4 marks

(a) Simplify (2⁵ × 2³) ÷ 2⁴. Give your answer as an ordinary number. (b) Find log₁₀(1000), explaining the power of 10 used.

### Question 4 — 4 marks

Six workers, each working 5 hours per day, complete a job in 8 days. How many days will 10 workers take if each works 6 hours per day? Assume every worker works at the same constant rate.

### Question 5 — 4 marks

A = [[2, -1], [3, 4]] and B = [[1, 5], [-2, 6]], where each inner bracket is a row. Calculate 2A - B. Write your answer in the same row format.

### Question 6 — 4 marks

A straight line passes through P(2, 5) and Q(6, 13). Find its gradient and its equation in the form y = mx + c.

### Question 7 — 4 marks

Solve 3(2x - 1) ≤ 21. Then list all the positive integer values of x that satisfy the inequality.

### Question 8 — 4 marks

A cylindrical container has an internal radius of 7 cm and an internal height of 10 cm. Calculate its capacity (a) in cm³ and (b) in litres. Use π = 22/7 and 1000 cm³ = 1 litre.

### Question 9 — 4 marks

A metal block has a mass of 540 g and a volume of 200 cm³. Calculate (a) its density in g/cm³ and (b) its weight in newtons. Use gravitational field strength g = 10 N/kg.

### Question 10 — 4 marks

A bus travels 84 km in 1 hour 24 minutes. Calculate its average speed (a) in km/h and (b) in m/s, correct to 1 decimal place.

### Question 11 — 4 marks

Two similar triangles have corresponding sides of 6 cm and 9 cm. The smaller triangle has an area of 24 cm². Find (a) the linear scale factor from the smaller triangle to the larger one and (b) the area of the larger triangle.

### Question 12 — 4 marks

The journey times of 10 learners are grouped as follows: 0 ≤ t < 10 minutes: 2 learners; 10 ≤ t < 20 minutes: 5 learners; 20 ≤ t < 30 minutes: 3 learners. (a) Estimate the mean journey time using class midpoints. (b) One of the 10 learners is chosen at random, with each equally likely to be chosen. Find the probability that their journey time is at least 20 minutes.

### Question 1

Answer: **3**

- Evaluate the brackets: -18 + 30 = 12. [1 mark]
- Divide 12 by -3 to get -4. [1 mark]
- Add -4 and 7. [1 mark]
- State the final answer 3. [1 mark]

### Question 2

Answer: **(a) 15 cm; (b) 1350 cm²**

- Use edge length = cube root of 3375. [1 mark]
- Obtain 15 cm. [1 mark]
- Use total surface area = 6 × 15². [1 mark]
- Obtain 1350 cm². [1 mark]

### Question 3

Answer: **(a) 16; (b) 3**

- Combine indices: 2^(5 + 3 - 4) = 2⁴. [1 mark]
- Evaluate 2⁴ = 16. [1 mark]
- State 1000 = 10³. [1 mark]
- Therefore log₁₀(1000) = 3. [1 mark]

### Question 4

Answer: **4 days**

- Calculate total work as 6 × 5 × 8 worker-hours. [1 mark]
- Obtain 240 worker-hours. [1 mark]
- New team's daily work is 10 × 6 = 60 worker-hours. [1 mark]
- Days = 240 ÷ 60 = 4. [1 mark]

### Question 5

Answer: **[[3, -7], [8, 2]]**

- Top-left entry: 2(2) - 1 = 3. [1 mark]
- Top-right entry: 2(-1) - 5 = -7. [1 mark]
- Bottom-left entry: 2(3) - (-2) = 8. [1 mark]
- Bottom-right entry: 2(4) - 6 = 2. [1 mark]

### Question 6

Answer: **Gradient 2; y = 2x + 1**

- Use gradient = (13 - 5) ÷ (6 - 2). [1 mark]
- Obtain gradient 2. [1 mark]
- Substitute (2, 5): 5 = 2(2) + c, so c = 1. [1 mark]
- State y = 2x + 1. [1 mark]

### Question 7

Answer: **x ≤ 4; positive integers: 1, 2, 3, 4**

- Expand to 6x - 3 ≤ 21. [1 mark]
- Obtain 6x ≤ 24. [1 mark]
- Divide by positive 6 to obtain x ≤ 4. [1 mark]
- List exactly 1, 2, 3 and 4; zero is not positive. [1 mark]

### Question 8

Answer: **(a) 1540 cm³; (b) 1.54 litres**

- Use V = πr²h. [1 mark]
- Substitute (22/7) × 7² × 10. [1 mark]
- Obtain 1540 cm³. [1 mark]
- Convert to 1540 ÷ 1000 = 1.54 litres. [1 mark]

### Question 9

Answer: **(a) 2.7 g/cm³; (b) 5.4 N**

- Density = mass ÷ volume = 540 ÷ 200. [1 mark]
- Obtain 2.7 g/cm³. [1 mark]
- Convert mass: 540 g = 0.54 kg. [1 mark]
- Weight = 0.54 × 10 = 5.4 N. [1 mark]

### Question 10

Answer: **(a) 60 km/h; (b) 16.7 m/s**

- Convert time: 1 + 24/60 = 1.4 hours. [1 mark]
- Average speed = 84 ÷ 1.4 = 60 km/h. [1 mark]
- Convert using 60 × 1000 ÷ 3600 m/s. [1 mark]
- Obtain 16.7 m/s to 1 decimal place. [1 mark]

### Question 11

Answer: **(a) 1.5; (b) 54 cm²**

- Use scale factor = 9 ÷ 6. [1 mark]
- Obtain 1.5. [1 mark]
- Area scale factor = 1.5² = 2.25. [1 mark]
- Larger area = 24 × 2.25 = 54 cm². [1 mark]

### Question 12

Answer: **(a) 16 minutes; (b) 3/10**

- Use midpoints 5, 15 and 25 minutes. [1 mark]
- Calculate weighted sum 2(5) + 5(15) + 3(25) = 160. [1 mark]
- Estimated mean = 160 ÷ 10 = 16 minutes. [1 mark]
- Probability = 3/10 (accept 0.3 or 30%). [1 mark]
